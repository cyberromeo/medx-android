# Bundled fonts

All three families are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and are bundled in `app/src/main/res/font`.

| File | Family | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `doto_bold.ttf`, `doto_black.ttf` | Doto (dot-matrix display), static instances of `Doto[ROND,wght].ttf` at ROND=100, wght=800/900 | github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/doto |
| `inter_*.ttf` | Inter (body) | Google Fonts (fontsource latin subset) |
| `space_mono_*.ttf` | Space Mono (labels) | Google Fonts (fontsource latin subset) |
